03Care in the room

Your people.
Your information.
Your reputation.

Our team is experienced in sensitive business environments. We understand that every interaction reflects on C3, as much as the films themselves.

People feel comfortable

We introduce ourselves, explain what we are filming and check that people are comfortable taking part. We respect anyone who prefers not to appear, adapt our position and approach, and keep the workshop flowing without pressure or disruption.

Information stays protected

Before filming, we agree what must stay off camera: confidential data, intellectual property, screens, documents and conversations. We choose angles carefully. If sensitive information appears in a selected film, we can discreetly blur, mask or black it out, or leave the shot out.

C3 is represented with care

We work with tact, discretion and professional judgment. We take care over how we speak to guests, how the team presents itself and what is captured. The finished films follow C3’s brand guidance and go through your review before delivery.

A clear day.
A clear delivery.

On the day

At WeWork Waterloo: setup from 07:30, filming until approximately 15:00 and packing away until 15:30. One video producer operates the camera and manages filming independently, fitting short interviews around the main activities. A C3 point of contact is available for practical questions during the day.

The videos

Two messages from C3 presenters, speaking directly to the camera (up to 60 seconds each), and two participant interviews (up to 90 seconds each). One overall event summary (approximately 1–2 minutes), with licensed music. Every finished video is supplied in both 16:9 widescreen and 4:5 LinkedIn formats, with on-screen English subtitles and a separate subtitle file. Individual interviews take up to ten minutes to record.

Your review

First versions within seven working days after filming and receipt of complete branding and permissions, whichever is later. Two combined rounds of feedback are included: one on the first versions, followed by a second for final refinements. We ask for one combined response from C3 at each stage, including any privacy corrections. Final files within two working days of the final feedback.
